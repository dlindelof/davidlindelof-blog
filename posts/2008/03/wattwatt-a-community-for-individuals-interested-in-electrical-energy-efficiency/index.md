[Wattwatt](http://wattwatt.com/) is a user-contributed website related to the efficiency of electrical energy in all its forms. It's a place to read blog entries, share ideas, and more generally participate in a growing community of energy-conscious people. I got this link through [Make magazine](http://www.makezine.com/)'s blog.

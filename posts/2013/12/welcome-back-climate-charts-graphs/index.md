date: 2013-12-04
---

I was happy to learn a few days ago that the [Climate Charts & Graphs](http://chartsgraphs.wordpress.com/) blog is being reactivated by its author. I used to subscribe to it back in the Google Reader days. In the current climate change conversation we need more blogs like CCG, where arguments can be conclusively settled with (preferably graphical) evidence.

So welcome back, and there's no reason to [apologise!](http://chartsgraphs.wordpress.com/2013/12/01/pardon-my-absence/)

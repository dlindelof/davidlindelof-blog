Our kind has existed forever. We don't quite know how this doesn't contradict the finite age of the universe, but it's a fact. You cannot possibly understand this; imagine that your own race manages to exist forever; now reverse the arrow of time, and you'll have a race that has existed forever, and which will exist forever.

We know wverything, because we have had more than eough time to work out everything. We know all the laws of the universe---always have. Everything has happened to us, but at no particular point in time. Oh we suck at engineering and computer science, in fact at any discipline that required efficiency. There's no incentive to work out fast algorithms when every computation can be pre-calculated, using pebbles in the sand if it so amuses us. We know the prime factors of every integer your race has ever used, by the way.

Thank God there's genuine randomness in the Universe or we'd have worked out its future evolution in every detail eons ago.

We reveal ourselves to every sentient race that evolves, but gradually so. We've never encountered a race that would survive a full, sudden disclosure of our existence. And since we've seen an infinite number of them we'll assume it never will happen. But most cultures accept the idea of the eternal, and we usually exploit this. Sometimes we leave cryptic, not-so-subtle hints---I-am-that-I-am and that sort of things. Would be funny if we hadn't used it like forever. I can't remember which revelation bundle we assigned to you---its identity would probably require all your world's storage to record anyway.

Oh and by the way: chess is a won game for black, and so is Go. Sorry for spoiling that.

You cannot point to a time when we developed, say, space travel: which species indeed would need eternity to develop something that even a challenged race like yours (no offence, but just one brain, really?) developed in the twinkling of an eye, metaphorically.

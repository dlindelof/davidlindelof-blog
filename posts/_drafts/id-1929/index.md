Here's one thing I've learned from 3+ years coaching the development team at Neurobat: you will never, ever, fulfil all the conditions required to be completely considered agile. Or doing XP. Or doing Scrum.

In our case, a key missing ingredient has always been the on-site Product Owner (PO). When we began Neurobat, the thought was that our founder would serve as de-facto PO, attending our bi-weekly demos over a shared desktop.

That worked for a few months. But eventually even that became infeasible, and our development team had to work without an on-site product owner.

In my pre-Neurobat experience, I have never, if ever, worked with a team where the PO was physically co-located with the development team (and that means being in the same room, not merely in the same building). So how do you deal with a PO who may be not closer than a phone call away (and that is roughly equivalent to having him on the dark side of the moon) ?

title: "Global warming doesn't matter"
draft: true
---

Imagine you're taking a swim in a normal-sized olympic pool. Suppose that this pool is full of people, who all of a sudden pee in your pool while you're in there.

Suppose, further, that instead of stopping, they begin to argue whether peeing in the pool really has any measurable effect on its temperature, or chemistry, or hygiene.

I would wager that you would feel very, very outraged that such a discussion would even be permitted to take place, instead of immediately stopping the offenders from continuing. Yet this is exactly what is going on with the current climate change discussion.

I'm sick and tired of listening to the debates on whether pumping CO2 in the atmosphere will increase the global temperature. I frankly don't care whether we know enough about the climate system to predict what will happen in the next decades. What I do care about is that we have, in a very short time, irresponsibly added huge amounts of a gas in the atmosphere without even realising it. There is no question that the amounts of atmospheric CO2 have increased since precise measurements began in the 50s. And there is no question that the current levels are way, way higher than any amounts recorded since geologic times.

We have all taken a collective pee in the pool. Instead of arguing about the possible effects, I suggest we stop peeing instead.

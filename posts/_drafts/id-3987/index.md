---
title: "Spend how many hours per week on personal growth again?"
draft: true
---

Recently I read an article on Medium about polymaths: people with a broad range of cross-disciplinary interests. The author of the article linked to an article according to which the secret sauce of these prodigies was to allocate at least 5 hours per week towards their personal growth.

I don't want to sound condescending, but I think that is an exceptionally low bar.

You are responsible for your own professional and personal growth. You are---not your employer, not your school. 5 hours per week is less than what some people spend on TV or video games---per day. Spending 5 hours per week learning new things is a ridiculously low threshold.

In "The Clean Coder", Robert Martin militates in favor of allocating 20 hours per week on your personal growth. That's slightly south of 3 hours per day.

Many agile methodologies insist on having the team agree, for each story, on a clear and unambiguous [definition of done (DoD)](https://www.scrum.org/Resources/Scrum-Glossary/Definition-of-Done).

Our experience at Neurobat has suggested that things are not always quite so simple. We have a distributed team, in the sense that the development team is in one location while the product management (including technical writer and product owner) are in another. We often have to make up for the absence of a co-located Product Owner by having someone stand as an informal proxy.

This also means that as a story gets worked on, we discover new requirements. We learn things about it.

---
title: "The greatest danger with a remote product owner"
draft: true
---

If you asked me the number one reason why a product owner should be co-located with the development team, I would say that it's the risk that the team should consider that person to be incompetent.

It's been documented that organizations with the smartest people tend to be the ones to develop the most pervasive, insidious not-invented-here syndrome. They tend to "flip the bozo bit" (McCarthy, Jim, _Dynamics of Software Development_) on other individuals and groups, sometimes for no other reason than that they are in another office, or building, or city.

Once this happens, there is of course less incentive for the team to collaborate with the product owner, and they will tend not to trust his or her work. There's also the risk that by declaring another person incompetent, the team may in fact be covering up their own poor collaboration skills.

If you lead a development team without a local product owner, be extremely sensitive to this kind of situation. Do not let this kind of distrust develop. Nip it in the bud. Insist on having the PO come and visit the team at regular intervals. Force him to use the phone instead of email. Setup a Skype account for him if you have to. The organization will thank you.

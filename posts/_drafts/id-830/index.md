We have always started from the beginning that Neurobat exists for only one purpose: reducing CO2 emissions resulting from the operation of buildings. The low-hanging fruit in this respect has always been the space heating.

According to [this FAQ](http://www.bafu.admin.ch/klima/09608/index.html?lang=fr#sprungmarke1_20) entry from the Swiss Federal Office for the Environment, the average carbon footprint of a person living in Switzerland is 12'800 kg of CO2 per year. Depending on the age of the dwelling, space heating accounts for between 1'200 kg (for a Minergie house) and 7'200 kg (for an older house). That's between 10% and more than 50%.

At Neurobat we measure the energy consumption for space heating on several different houses, and have been doing so for the past couple of years. In fact we have a pretty good idea about the relationship between climate factors and how much heating will be required by the house.

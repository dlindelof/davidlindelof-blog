There are apps out there to help you form new habits, presumably predicated on the idea that a new habit needs about 3 weeks to form. Only problem is, those apps never worked for me.

Four times, at least, over the past 5 years, I' ve given a shot at using a habit-forming app to, for example, help me write morning pages or work on open source projects. And each time, I eventually just forgot to check-in and gave up on the habit.

I've now discovered the key to using those apps succesfully. It's a simple truth that's obvious when you think about it, but which makes all the differente. Here it is: the reguler use of a jabit forming app is a habit itself that must be formed.

If you use such an app to form just one or two habits, the result is that you won't be using it enough for it to become part of your daily workflow. And that's exactly what I did wrong. Now, instead of trying to form just one of two habits, I track a dozen:

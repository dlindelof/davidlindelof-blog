title: "Why we chose to use PostgreSQL at Neurobat"
draft: true
---

New team members at Neurobat are sometimes perplexed by my choice of our main relational database management system. They are used to the classic LAMP stack and seem to believe that The One True Open-Source RDBM has to be MySQL.

Here are some reasons that motivated my choice of PostgreSQL, several years ago.

- It supports more of the official SQL standard
    
- It works as expected out of the box
    
- It is not associated with a big company

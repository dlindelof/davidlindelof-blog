TO BE FLESHED OUT

A thermal controller buys or imposes taxes on heat fluxes. A lighting controller provides solar gains at a lower price than a heating actuator, so the market favors the use of solar gains.

At night, the thermal controller must anticipate the evolution of future heat flux costs in order to effectively pre-heat (or not) the regulated zone.

Zone controllers know the thermal resistivity between zones (a more general concept than rooms).

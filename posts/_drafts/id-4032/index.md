I have seen providence at work ever since that fateful winter day when our CEO announced that the board was shutting down the company. With hindsight, I realize how perfect the timing was, and how it ended up benefitting everyone.

My wife was pregnant with twins at the time, due for delivery in Apris 2018. Out place was very ill-equipped to welcome baby twins; being able to enjoy three months at home was moer than fantastic. And being able to spend most of my time at home close to my family after the birth is a blessing few people have.

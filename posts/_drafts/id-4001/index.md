Going through my old Neurobat files, I came across an interesting dataset. It's the daily average temperature for several localities in Switzerland for the past several years. Plotting the data, and adding a linear trend, brings to life something we've been told for a long time but probably haven't internalized: that climate change happens to Switzerland much faster than the global average:

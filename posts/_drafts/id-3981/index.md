The important points for a newly minted team manager / engineering lead:

- Technical work likely to be limited to bug fixes and small features
- Identify bottlenecks in the process and readblocks, and clear these roadblocks
- Identify the most high-value projects of the organisation, and keep the team focused on these projects
- Identify headcount need; plan and recruit to fill these needs
- Solicit and deliver frequent feedback
- Communicate timeline, scope, and risks to pillar partners
- Identify areas of strategic technical debt, do the cost/benefit analysis for resolving the debt
- Stay technical! Or you won't be able to do most of the points above

title: "The DEBORAH project kick-off meeting"
date: 2016-06-20

We are involved in DEBORAH, a Eurostars project nr E!10286,  led by [EQUA Simulation AB](http://www.equa.se/en/), the vendor of the highly regarded [IDA ICE](http://www.equa.se/en/ida-ice) building simulation software. Together with [CSEM](https://www.csem.ch/home) and [Bengt Dahlgren AB](http://bengtdahlgren.se/), a Swedish consultancy firm specialised in buildings, the project's stated objective is to optimise the design and operation of district thermal energy systems.

We held the project's kick-off meeting on Thursday 16th June, 2016, in EQUA's offices in Stockholm. Neurobat's role in the project will consist in providing short- and long-term estimates of heating loads, and to extend IDA ICE with the Neurobat control algorithms.

A pilot site has been identified in [Krokslätt](https://sv.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kroksl%C3%A4tt), a district in the city of Göteborg, where heating to several buildings is provided by heat pumps combined with a system of [boreholes](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Borehole): narrow shafts drilled through the rocky ground, where the water fed to the heat pumps have their temperature raised by the surrounding heat. Besides "pre-heating" the water, this also has the benefit of improving the heat pump's [coefficient of performance (COP)](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coefficient_of_performance). But few studies have been done regarding the optimal design (and operation) of such a system of boreholes, a negligence that this project hopes to address.

This 3-year long project is a great opportunity for us to work with some of the domain's thought leaders, and to integrate IDA ICE in our own product development workflow.
